# Iteration 06: career notebook

Built on `2d07974` (production). Branch `feature/crvro-3d-career-notebook`, worktree `~/Projects/crvro-3d-career`. Not merged, not pushed, not deployed. No backend or environment-variable changes. The two older `claude/zarvin-*` branches on the remote (ten weeks old, one commit each) were not touched; this work shares no files with them.

## What was built
- **A career ledger on the desk** (`src/workspace3d/notebook.js`): a cloth-bound field book with copper corners, a typed label, an amber ribbon, ruled and gridded paper. Built once from boxes and canvas textures, added to the scene (so `engine.dispose()` releases it), driven by four numbers (cover angle, three leaf angles). The cover swings open and the leaves turn; nothing animates when it is still.
- **Placement from the camera framing, not from taste.** The desk's left end is free and framed on landscape screens, so the book lies there (clear of the loose disk, the organizer and the pencil cup). On a portrait phone that corner is outside the frame at every look angle (checked at every look angle: it is off screen, or hidden behind the computer), but the wall shelf's free right half is inside it, so on portrait the book stands cover-out on the shelf and is carried down to the desk spot to be read. Which place it uses follows the same `max-aspect-ratio: 4/5` rule as the phone's rest camera. Resizing across that line moves a closed, idle book.
- **A reading surface that is real text.** The words are DOM (`src/components/CareerContent.jsx`) laid over the open book's right-hand page, placed from the page's projected corners and clipped to its outline, on paper styled like the 3D leaves. The 3D leaves carry no text that matters. This keeps the type crisp, selectable and screen-reader readable at 390 px (body type 14 px and up) while the page is still visibly a page of the book. The surface appears only after the camera has arrived (`st.fit`), is re-fit when the dock or the window changes size, and is removed the moment the camera leaves.
- **One career data file** (`src/data/career.js`) feeds the notebook, the reading surface and the LO-FI page. Withheld and conflicting facts are in `CAREER_REVIEW` (`docs/career-source.md`).
- **Routing and controller, no second engine.** New state `book` in the existing controller (`st.page`: work, highlights, resume). `/#career`, `/#career/highlights`, `/#career/resume`; an unknown page falls back to `/#career`. `stationOfUi`, `stationFromHash`, the station effects, Back/Forward and the case-study "back" origin all go through the existing station code. A fifth station, "Career notebook", joins the switcher and the phone Stations list.
- **Interaction.** Click or tap the book (hover says what it is) → camera over the desk spot, cover opens, page 1. Page tabs on the paper, Prev/Next in the dock, ←/→ (past the first or last page the arrows move on to the neighbouring station), N, Esc, Back to the desk, a tap on the desk beside the book. A drag that starts on the book looks around and does not open it. Dragging inside the open book does not close it. A touch drag on the page scrolls the page. Leaving by any route closes the book (the cover is driven from the controller's state, not from the action that left).
- **LO-FI.** `/#career` is a plain, semantic page (`CareerPage.jsx`) that never loads the engine. The home page links to it. HI-FI↔LO-FI keeps the page. `?view=2d` is now read on the first render, so asking for the plain page no longer downloads the 3D chunk first (the choice is still saved).
